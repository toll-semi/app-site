Privacy Policy (iOS)

Updated / Effective: October 18, 2026

Tollsemi Charger (the "App") is operated by the developer. This policy applies to the iOS version of the App. We value your privacy and personal information and provide this Privacy Policy in accordance with applicable laws.

1. Information We Collect

  1. Device information needed to connect chargers over Bluetooth and show status, including model, OS version, Bluetooth connection state, and charger identifiers.
  2. Permission-related data:
    • Bluetooth — scan and connect charger BLE devices and sync status/data;
    • Camera/Photos — always-on display wallpapers and video assets;
    • Photo library write — save always-on display videos for Shortcuts;
    • Notifications — charging status and Live Activities where applicable.
  3. Local storage: device list, always-on display wallpaper/video selection, and app settings stored on device.

The iOS version of the App currently does not require an account and does not collect phone numbers, emails, or other registration/login credentials.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information only to:

  • Provide core features (Bluetooth device link, charge data, always-on display wallpapers and videos);
  • Protect service security, troubleshoot issues, and improve the product;
  • Send necessary service notices with your consent.

3. Storage and Protection

  1. The App does not collect your personal information to a server; related data is stored only on your phone locally.
  2. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures against leakage, loss, or unauthorized access.
  3. You may delete added devices in-app; uninstalling clears local data.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. Sharing may occur only when:

  • You give explicit consent;
  • Required by law, courts, or regulators;
  • Necessary to protect users or the public.

5. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete personal information and revoke permissions. Disabling camera, Bluetooth, Photos, or notifications in system settings may limit some features.

6. Children

If you are a minor, read this policy and use the App under guardian guidance.

7. Updates

We may revise this policy and publish updates in the App or on this page. Continued use means you accept the revised policy.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us via in-app feedback or published developer channels.
