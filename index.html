关于我们

深圳市拓尔微电子有限责任公司（Tollsemi）专注充电与电源相关电子产品。Tollsemi Charger 是公司推出的官方配套应用，用于连接充电器、查看充电状态，并管理表盘、壁纸与充电秀等内容。

产品功能

  • USB / 蓝牙连接充电器，识别设备并同步状态
  • 查看电压、电流、功率、温度与电池信息
  • 功率分配与充电模式设置
  • 表盘、壁纸、屏保与充电秀内容传输
  • 固件升级与设备信息管理

About

Shenzhen Tollsemi Electronics Co., Ltd. develops charging and power electronics products. Tollsemi Charger is the official companion app for connecting chargers, viewing charging status, and managing watch faces, wallpapers, and charge-show content.

Features

  • Connect chargers over USB or Bluetooth and sync device status
  • View voltage, current, power, temperature, and battery data
  • Configure power allocation and charging modes
  • Transfer watch faces, wallpapers, screensavers, and charge-show content
  • Firmware updates and device information
